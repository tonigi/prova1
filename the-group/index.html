The group

Group members, alumni, and mentoring activities.

The group includes the PI, yours truly, and students with a passion for AI, computational modeling, statistics, and affine topics. Lab members belong to several institutions.

Current members

  • I. Cazzaniga, Research Fellow (CNR)
  • F. Bacic Toplek, Post-doctoral research collaborator (CNR)
  • S. Naik, Pre-doctoral research collaborator (CNR)

Where we work

We are located in the Department of Biosciences of the University of Milan. Due to the nature of computational work, we collaborate both in person and online.

Joining opportunities

  • Interested in joining? Drop me an email.
  • There are usually opportunities for theses (final triennale/specialistica), and occasionally PhD.
  • Selection procedures are advertised in the official CNR website (see e.g. sections Concorsi e opportunità, Selezioni online and Concorsi e selezioni).
